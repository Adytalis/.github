# Security Policy

Adytalis builds software that people trust with their work and their accounts. We welcome reports from anyone who finds a weakness in an Adytalis app or service.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't report security problems in public issues, discussions or pull requests.**

- If the app's repository has its own `SECURITY.md`, follow it: it names the right contact.
- For **Adexix**, email **security@adexix.com**.

Please include the app and version, what you found, how to reproduce it, and its impact as you see it.

## What happens next

| Stage | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement | Within 3 working days |
| Initial assessment | Within 10 working days |
| Fix for a critical or high-severity issue | Within 30 days of confirmation |

We aim for coordinated disclosure within 90 days, agreed with you, and credit reporters who want it.

## Safe harbour

Adytalis will not pursue legal action against anyone who researches and reports a vulnerability in good faith: testing only their own accounts and installations, accessing no more data than needed to show the problem, not disrupting services, and giving us reasonable time to fix it before telling anyone else.
