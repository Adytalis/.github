# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Adytalis apps are built by people and AI agents working together, and used by people who trust them with their ideas and their work. We want everyone who takes part in the project to be able to do their best work in an environment that is respectful, welcoming and free from harassment.

We commit to making participation in Adytalis projects a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in every Adytalis space: every Adytalis repository and its issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions; project documents and decision pages; email and chat about Adytalis apps; and events where someone represents the project. It also applies to the instructions people give to AI agents working on Adytalis projects, and to what those agents write on their behalf. A person is responsible for the conduct of the agents they direct.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Critique ideas, code and designs directly and specifically.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.
- Give credit accurately. Never present someone else's work, words or decisions as your own, and never attribute to someone a decision or view they did not state.
- Be honest about what you have done and checked. Claim only what you can show.
- Accept responsibility for your mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment of any kind, in public or private.
- Insults, slurs, or derogatory comments about a person or group.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Threats of violence, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a home address or private email, without their explicit permission.
- Sharing other people's data, credentials or confidential project material outside the project.
- Knowingly misrepresenting work, test results or decisions.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to **conduct@adexix.com** (until Adytalis has its own address). Reports go only to the Adytalis maintainers. If a report concerns one of the maintainers, say so in the email and it will be handled by Adytalis without that person's involvement.

Please include what happened, where and when, who was involved, and any links or screenshots. You can report anonymously, although that may limit what we can do.

Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. We will acknowledge your report within 5 working days. We will keep the reporter's identity and the details confidential, sharing them only as far as the investigation requires. Nobody will face retaliation for making a report in good faith.

## Enforcement

The Adytalis maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow it, and they will explain their decision when appropriate.

When deciding on a response, the maintainers will consider the impact on the people affected and whether the behaviour is part of a pattern:

| Level | Behaviour | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Correction | Inappropriate language or other behaviour that is unprofessional or unwelcome. | A private, written warning explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested. |
| 2. Warning | A single serious incident, or repeated minor ones. | A warning with conditions, such as no contact with the people involved for a set period. Breaking the conditions may lead to a temporary or permanent ban. |
| 3. Temporary ban | A serious breach, including sustained inappropriate behaviour. | A temporary ban from all interaction and contribution in Adytalis spaces. |
| 4. Permanent ban | A pattern of breaches, harassment of an individual, or aggression towards or disparagement of any group. | A permanent ban from all Adytalis spaces. |

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour is expected to stop immediately.
